TMNT 1.1.2: WIDE's skateboards end again, and what the game takes away at the screen's edges goes by WIDE's sides.
As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players)
beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Fixed**

- **WIDE, the skateboards' road** (issue 30, on the Steam Deck and the Switch): after the first helicopters, the
  ones that come in at the left were never seen and never gone, their shadows going by again and again, and the
  road never ended. They were put past the wider picture's left side, where the game read their place as past the
  right edge: they flew away from the turtles instead of to them. They come in from the left side now, fly to the
  turtles and go, and the road ends as in 4:3.

**Changed**

- **WIDE, the game's screen edges**: every place where the game takes something away, stops it or keeps it in at a
  side of the screen now goes by WIDE's sides, all at once, instead of the arcade's 4:3 edges. What vanished in
  sight in the sides now leaves by the picture's edge: the Mousers carrying Splinter off at the building site's
  start, the highway's Foot car, the Technodrome's balls; the flying drones no longer turn back in sight at the
  left. The bosses' walls and the rock soldier's missile keep the arcade's limits where the bosses have no room.
  4:3 plays exactly as before. Checked by running every area in 16:9, 20:9 and 16:10 on a computer; tell us if
  anything still pops out of sight in the sides.

Each package's `README.txt` says what it needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
