The first release of the TMNT port: Konami's 1989 arcade game *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles* as a native game,
built on its decompilation ([tmntarcade-decomp](https://github.com/cheviman/tmntarcade-decomp)) and checked frame
by frame against MAME. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World
4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**The packages** (Windows, Linux, macOS for Apple silicon and Intel) hold both games and the tools that make the
devices' games with your ROM set:

- **TMNT**: the console version. No coins: START brings a player in at any time, up to four. ARCADE, SPLINTER WAY
  (NORMAL with 5 lives and 3 continues, BOSS RUSH, RONIN), LAN, OPTIONS (CONTROLS for four players, DIFFICULTY,
  LIVES, DEMO SOUND, REGION, VIDEO with PIXELS, WIDE 16:9 or 20:9 and FPS, VOLUME, SOUND TEST), the pause
  (CONTINUE, RESTART, QUIT GAME). And a certain code on the menu.
- **TMNT Arcade**: the arcade game as it is: coins, the test mode, four players.
- **TMNT NSP** (Nintendo Switch: TMNT.nsp and TMNT Arcade.nsp, with your console's keys), **TMNT APK** (Android:
  TMNT.apk and TMNT Arcade.apk, with a key of yours; touch controls or game controllers) and **TMNT Anbernic** (the
  H700 handhelds' PORTS).

**LAN**: up to four computers on the same local network play one game, a turtle each: one hosts a room, the others
join it, the host's START begins it on all of them with the host's settings. A computer that stops being heard
leaves after 3 seconds, and the game goes on. It is built on
[arcade-lan](https://github.com/cheviman/arcade-lan).

**Not tried yet**: a real Switch, an Anbernic handheld, a Mac, and the LAN between two computers (it was played
with two games on one). Reports are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/cheviman/tmnt-port/issues).

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
