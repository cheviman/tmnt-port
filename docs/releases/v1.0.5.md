TMNT 1.0.5: Android TV and Google TV, and 32-bit Android. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages:
keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the
first time it starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**New**

- **32-bit Android** (armeabi-v7a): the APKs that TMNT APK makes have the game for both kinds of Android, 64-bit and
  32-bit, and Android installs the one its device runs. A 32-bit Android did not install them before: the Chromecast
  with Google TV (its Android is 32-bit on a 64-bit chip), TV boxes and older phones. The 18 scenarios give the same
  game as the board's, frame by frame, on 32-bit ARM too.
- **In the TV's apps** (Android TV, Google TV): the games show up there, with a banner, the four turtles' faces from
  the same friend's mural as the icon. A 16:9 picture of your own takes its place: drop it on TMNT APK's window
  (`banner.jpg` beside the tool, or `--banner` on a command line).
- **The TV's remote**, in TMNT: the pad moves, OK attacks (and brings in a player who is not playing, or continues),
  back jumps (and goes back in the menus), home pauses. A game controller comes first, as a player, and is better.
  In TMNT Arcade, as before, back puts a coin in.

Not yet tried on a Chromecast or an Android TV: installing, the banner, the remote and the speed are to be seen
there.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
