TMNT 1.1.1: the Switch starts again, BOSS RUSH's cards, and a faster picture on slow devices. As before, nothing of
the game comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it
takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Fixed**

- **The Switch** (issue 27): 1.1.0 stopped at its start on every Switch, "The window cannot be opened: EGL not
  initialized", TMNT and TMNT Arcade both. The window now asks for what devkitPro's SDL 3 needs to open it. Built,
  but not yet tried on a Switch: the picture docked and handheld, the sound and the controllers are still the first
  things to tell. If it does not start on yours, 1.0.7 is the one that does.
- **BOSS RUSH's cards** (issue 24): with VSYNC on (the default), the picture stood still while the turtles crossed
  each area, the card with the area and its boss never shown, and the boss's fight came out of nowhere. The card is
  on the screen through the crossing again.
- **The turtle choice's song** with DEMO SOUND off: the menus' song went on over it instead of the choice's own.
- **A LAN game's end** gave the device its own DIP switches back (DEMO SOUND, the lives, the difficulty, the coins),
  where the game kept the host's until OPTIONS changed one.

**Changed**

- **Slow devices** (the Switch, the Anbernic, a Raspberry Pi 3): the game draws its picture some 13 times faster,
  the same picture pixel for pixel. Where drawing a frame took longer than its time, the game and its sound slowed
  down together; a frame now leaves most of its time free. No frame is left out. Measured on a computer (from 2.4 ms
  a frame to 0.2 in WIDE 16:9); tell how it goes on a device, and whether the sound still breaks up anywhere.
