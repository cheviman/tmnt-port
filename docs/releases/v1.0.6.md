TMNT 1.0.6: the first players' reports, and the devices in packages of their own. As before, nothing of the game
comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes
its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Fixed**

- **The pause stood still** (TMNT, issues 2 and 9): with VSYNC on, the pause kept showing its first picture, so
  the line chosen moved but the screen did not, and a game controller seemed not to answer. It now keeps the
  frames' pace, as the rest of the game.
- **The pause by each player's controls** (issue 4): it reads what each player set in CONTROLS, the left stick and
  the touch screen too, as the other menus do: the button that is ATTACK picks, JUMP goes back.
- **WIDE through the continue** (issue 10): the CONTINUE countdown and its GAME OVER stay in widescreen, their texts
  in the picture's middle, and PRESS START and GAME OVER under the boxes with them.
- **macOS's own full screen** (issue 8): with the window's green button, a size changed in OPTIONS put the picture
  at the top left of the screen. Not yet tried on a Mac.
- **BOSS RUSH** (issue 3): the crossing to each boss no longer ends early (Raphael and Donatello attacked so often
  they hardly walked), the crossing no longer raises the difficulty, and the highway's and the skateboards' card
  shows the next boss.
- **WIDE 20:9 after a boss fight** (issue 3): a turtle could end up past the screen's left edge, and area 3's exit
  hung there, the turtle walking left for ever.
- **The parked cars' barrel at Bebop and Rocksteady**, in WIDE's sides: it no longer takes Bebop's colours.
- **A game controller plugged in before the game** started was left a plain joystick, without its mapping.

**New**

- **INTEGER SCALE** in OPTIONS > VIDEO (issue 12): the picture scaled by whole numbers only, black around it.
- **PRESS START** in OPTIONS > VIDEO (issue 11): BLINK (as the arcade), ON or OFF. The continue's countdown and a
  MULTIPLAYER game still blink, as the board does.
- **HEALTH INFINITE** in the Konami code's page: the turtles' health kept full.

**Changed**

- **Windows: Direct3D 11** shows the picture, in place of Direct3D 9 (`SDL_RENDER_DRIVER` still chooses another).
  The game itself is the same: the board's video is drawn by the game, the renderer only shows each frame.
- **macOS: two packages** (issue 7): `tmnt-macos-universal.zip` with TMNT and `tmnt-arcade-macos-universal.zip` with
  TMNT Arcade, one app each, so a launcher can point at either one.
- **The devices' packages**, apart from the computers' ones: `tmnt-switch.zip`, `tmnt-android.zip` and
  `tmnt-anbernic.zip`, each with the tool that makes your own games for the device, for Windows, macOS and Linux.
  The Switch's and Anbernic's also have the games without their assets, ready to take them out of your `tmnt.zip`
  on the device itself: the Switch's `.nro` for the Homebrew Menu, with `tmnt.zip` in `/switch/tmnt/`, and
  Anbernic's folder for `Roms/PORTS`, with `tmnt.zip` in `PORTS/tmnt/`. Android's has TMNT APK alone this time.
  The computers' packages carry the two games alone.

Not yet tried on the devices themselves: the first start taking the assets out of `tmnt.zip` on a Switch and an
Anbernic is to be seen there.

Each package's `README.txt` says what it needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
