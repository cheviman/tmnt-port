TMNT 1.0.2: VSYNC. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM
set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices'
games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**New**

- **VSYNC** (OPTIONS > VIDEO, on by default, kept in `tmnt.ini`): each frame is shown at the screen's refresh, with
  no tearing. The game keeps its own pace, the board's 59.19 frames a second: a faster screen (120 Hz, 144 Hz)
  shows a frame more than once, and a frame that comes late is left out rather than holding the game or its sound.
  On a computer; the Switch, Android and the Anbernic keep their system's own refresh. TMNT Arcade is as it was.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
