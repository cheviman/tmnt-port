TMNT 1.0.4: a Fold opened mid-game, and the icon. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your
own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it
starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **A Fold opened or closed mid-game** (Android): the picture kept the shape it was chosen for at the start, and on
  the open screen it took half of it. WIDE now follows the screen as it changes: 20:9 on the closed Fold, 16:9 on
  the open one, the picture at the top and the touch controls under it (a tablet's screen, its short side 4 inches
  or more, does the same). The touch controls take the new screen's density too. Not in a MULTIPLAYER game, where
  WIDE is the host's on every device. Not yet tried on a Fold.

**Changed**

- **The icon** is Raphael's face from a friend's mural, on every platform: the computers' executables and .apps,
  the Android launcher (what TMNT APK makes without a picture of yours) and the Switch's HOME menu. A picture of
  your own still takes its place: drop it on TMNT APK's or TMNT NSP's window.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
