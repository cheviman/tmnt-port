TMNT 1.0.1: the whole screen on Android. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own
`tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it
starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **Full screen on Android**: TMNT Arcade opened as a window, so Android's bars stayed over the game, and on
  Android 15, which draws an app under them, the score at the top was cut (a player's OnePlus 12). Both games now
  take the whole screen, the bars hidden until a swipe from the edge brings them back. TMNT Arcade keeps the
  cabinet's 4:3 picture, black at its sides; TMNT's WIDE fills the phone.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
