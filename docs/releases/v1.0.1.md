TMNT 1.0.1: the whole screen on Android, game controllers before a TV's remote, and the players' boxes in the
middle of WIDE. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own
`tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it
starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **Full screen on Android**: TMNT Arcade opened as a window, so Android's bars stayed over the game, and on
  Android 15, which draws an app under them, the score at the top was cut (a player's OnePlus 12). Both games now
  take the whole screen, the bars hidden until a swipe from the edge brings them back. TMNT Arcade keeps the
  cabinet's 4:3 picture, black at its sides; TMNT's WIDE fills the phone.
- **A game controller on Android TV** (a Shield with an Xbox controller): the TV's remote, which Android reports as
  a game controller and comes first, took player 1, and the controller played player 2. A game controller now
  comes before a remote, whatever their order.
- **The players' boxes in WIDE** (16:9 and 20:9): at an area's start the picture's sides are all at the right (so
  that nothing before the area's map shows), and the boxes at the top moved with the board's 320 columns, back to
  the middle as the scroll went on. They stay in the middle now.

**Changed**

- The menu's LAN is MULTIPLAYER: the same game of up to four computers on the local network.
- The repository's README is the port's alone, short: playing it, the console version, the controls, LAN,
  building it. How the port is made is in `docs/port.md`, and the decompilation in tmntarcade-decomp's README.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
