TMNT 1.1.0: SDL 3, the Raspberry Pi, and iPhone and iPad. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages:
keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4 Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the
first time it starts.

**New**

- **Raspberry Pi**: `tmnt-raspberrypi-arm64.tar.gz`, TMNT and TMNT Arcade for a Raspberry Pi 3 or later (3, Zero 2 W,
  4, 400, 5, 500) with the 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS, Debian 12 "Bookworm" or later; the 32-bit Raspberry Pi OS does not
  run it. Start `./TMNT` or `"./TMNT Arcade"` from the folder, in the desktop or with none (Raspberry Pi OS Lite,
  where the game takes the whole screen). The game plays the same frame by frame under emulation, the first run
  from `tmnt.zip` included. Not yet tried on a Raspberry Pi: the picture, the sound, the controllers and the speed on
  a Pi 3 are to be seen there.
- **iPhone and iPad**: `tmnt-ios.zip`, the two games as .ipa files for iOS and iPadOS 13 or later. They are not on
  the App Store, and never will be: you install them yourself (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly, which sign them
  with your own Apple ID; the package's README says how) and put `tmnt.zip` in the game's folder in the Files app.
  The touch screen's controls as on Android, or a game controller. Built and started in the iOS Simulator by the
  release's build; not yet tried on an iPhone or iPad, nor played there. If a game does not start, send `tmnt.log`
  from its folder in an issue. MULTIPLAYER is not expected to find the rooms there yet.

**Changed**

- **SDL 3** (the window, the sound and the controls: SDL 3.4.20 where SDL 2 was) on Windows, Linux, macOS and
  Android. The game is the same frame by frame; what a player may notice:
  - On Windows the window is at the screen's own pixels. On a desktop scaled to 125% or 150% it is smaller and sharp
    where it was stretched and soft; OPTIONS > VIDEO > WINDOW makes it larger.
  - On a computer a Nintendo controller's buttons go by their place, as an Xbox controller's: the button at the
    bottom attacks, the one at the right jumps.
  Not yet played through by hand on SDL 3: if the sound, the full screen, VSYNC, the pause or a controller is not as
  it was, tell in an issue, with `tmnt.log`.
- **The Nintendo Switch on SDL 3**: devkitPro has no SDL 3 of its own yet, so the games carry its branch of it (SDL
  3.4.0, not released). Not yet tried on a Switch: the picture docked and handheld, the sound and the controllers
  are the first things to tell. If it does not start on yours, 1.0.7 is the one that does.
- **Anbernic**: its games keep the firmware's SDL 2 under the same source. Not yet tried on a handheld.
- **Linux**: the package asks the system for nothing new; SDL finds PipeWire by itself now, beside PulseAudio and
  ALSA.

**Fixed**

- **Donatello lands with his bo** (issue 23): falling into areas 2, 3 and 7, his landing pose had a piece of
  Raphael's sai where the bo's lower end goes (one bit of the arcade board's own data; MAME shows it too). TMNT puts
  it right; TMNT Arcade keeps the board's data.

Each package's `README.txt` says what it needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
