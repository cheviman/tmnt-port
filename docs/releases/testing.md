TMNT testing: the next release's changes, for the players to try before it comes out. It is not a release: it is
made again at each change, and something in it may not work yet. Tell what you find in the issues
(github.com/cheviman/tmnt-port/issues), with the version TMNT's OPTIONS shows at the bottom right (V...-TEST...).
As in a release, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `tmnt.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World 4
Players) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**To try**

- WIDE, the skateboards' road (issue 30): the helicopters that come in at the left fly in to the turtles and go,
  and the road ends. They were put past the wider picture's left side and flew on away from it, never seen and
  never gone, their shadows going by again and again with no end to the road.
- WIDE, the game's screen edges: every place where the game takes something away, stops it or keeps it in at a side
  of the screen now goes by WIDE's sides, all at once, instead of the arcade's 4:3 edges. What vanished in sight in
  the sides leaves by the picture's edge: the Mousers carrying Splinter off at the building site's start, the
  highway's Foot car, the Technodrome's balls; the flying drones no longer turn back in sight at the left; and the
  bosses' walls and the rock soldier's missile keep the arcade's limits where the bosses have no room. Tell us if
  anything still pops out of sight in the sides.
